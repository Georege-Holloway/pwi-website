What we make

Five things, all made to measure in our workshop and fitted by us.

Fitted wardrobes

Wardrobes made to measure for the space you have, rather than bought in fixed widths and packed out with filler panels. We work to the actual dimensions of the room, including the sloped ceilings, the skirting and the chimney breast that stops an off the shelf unit from fitting.

What goes inside is planned around what you own. Common arrangements include:

  • Long hanging for coats and dresses
  • Double hanging for shirts, jackets and folded items
  • Banks of drawers, internal or external
  • Shoe racking and shelving
  • Full wall runs with the storage carried over the bed or a doorway

Alcoves

The recesses either side of a chimney breast are usually the most wasted space in a room. Built in properly they give you storage without taking a single extra centimetre of floor space.

We build bookcases, cupboards and contemporary floating shelving into alcoves, with cupboards below and open shelving above being the most common arrangement. Alcove joinery is also the easiest way to conceal pipework, meter boxes and wiring that cannot be moved.

Media units

A media unit has to hold a television, a soundbar or speakers, a games console, a router and whatever else has accumulated, and it has to do it without a nest of cables hanging down the wall.

We design the cable routes and the ventilation in from the start rather than drilling holes afterwards, and we build the unit to suit the wall it sits on. Done well it becomes the focal point of the room instead of the thing you try not to look at.

Home offices

Working from home covers a wide range of setups, and so does this. At one end, a spare bedroom fitted out properly with a desk, drawers, shelving and cable management. At the other, a compact desk and storage built into the space under the stairs.

Either way it is built to the dimensions of the space and to the way you actually work, which is the part that a flat pack desk cannot do.

General carpentry

Alongside the fitted furniture we take on second fix carpentry. That includes hanging internal doors and building under stairs storage.

If you are unsure whether a job falls within what we do, call and ask. We would rather tell you honestly that it is not for us than take it on badly.
