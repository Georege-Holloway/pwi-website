About us

Two carpenters, one workshop, and furniture built for the room it goes in.

Richard and Michael

Pro Woodwork Installations is run by two people, Richard and Michael. Between them they have more than forty years of experience in the trade. When you call, you speak to one of them. When the furniture is measured, designed, built and fitted, it is done by the same two people throughout.

The workshop

We work out of our own workshop in the Bromley area. That is where the cutting, assembly and finishing happen, which is the main practical difference between us and a carpenter who works entirely on site.

It means we are not carrying a table saw through your hallway, and it means the quality of the finish is not limited by what can be achieved in a bedroom with dust sheets down.

What we take on, and what we do not

We are a bespoke joinery firm, not a general building contractor. Our work is fitted furniture, wardrobes, alcove units, media units and home offices, plus second fix carpentry such as hanging doors and under stairs storage.
