What we collect

If you fill in the enquiry form on this site we collect the name, email address, phone number and postcode you enter, your choice of service, and whatever you write in the message field. We do not collect anything else through this website. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry and, if it goes further, to quote for and carry out work for you. The lawful basis is legitimate interests, specifically responding to someone who has contacted us asking about our services, and where we go on to work together, performance of a contract.

Who else sees it

This website is hosted by Netlify, and the enquiry form is handled by Netlify Forms, provided by Netlify Inc. Form submissions are stored on Netlify's systems, which includes servers outside the United Kingdom, and are forwarded to our email. Netlify acts as a data processor on our behalf. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing.
