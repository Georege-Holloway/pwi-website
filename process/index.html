How it works

From the first phone call to the finished furniture, and what happens in between.

From enquiry to installation

  1. You get in touch

    Call Richard or Michael, or send an email with the room, roughly what you want and your postcode. The postcode matters because it tells us straight away whether you are within the area we cover.

  2. We survey and measure at your home

    We come to the house and measure the space properly. Fitted furniture lives or dies on the survey, because walls are rarely square, floors are rarely level and skirting boards are never where you would like them to be. We also use the visit to talk through what will work in the room and what will not.

  3. Design and quote

    We come back to you with what we propose to build and what it will cost.

Every buyer in this category asks what it costs, and the question does not go away just because the site does not answer it.

  4. We build it at the workshop

    Once the design is agreed, your furniture is manufactured at our workshop. Carcasses, doors, drawers and shelving are cut, assembled and finished there, off site and away from your house. This is the part of the process that keeps the mess out of your home.

    Placeholder: lead times, OPEN-5

    How long does the workshop build take, typically, from agreed design to installation date? A real range, even a wide one, is far better than silence.

  5. Installation

    We deliver the finished units and fit them ourselves. Scribing to the walls, hanging the doors, fitting the handles and making good is done on site, which is unavoidable, but it is a fraction of the work and therefore a fraction of the disruption. We clear up after ourselves before we leave.

Common questions

Straight answers to what people usually ask before committing to a project.

Do you make everything in your own workshop?

Yes. Your furniture is manufactured offsite at our workshop and brought to your home finished. Only the installation itself happens in the house, which means far less dust, noise and disruption than furniture built entirely on site.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across London and the South East from a workshop base in the Bromley area.
