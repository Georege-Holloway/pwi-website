What we make

Five things we do, and we do them properly

We are a two man joinery firm. Everything below is designed for the room it goes in, built by us, and fitted by us.

Fitted wardrobes

Made to measure for the space you have. Drawers, double hanging, long hanging and shelving arranged around what you actually own, from a single alcove to a full wall of storage.

Fitted wardrobes

Alcoves

Bookcases, cupboards and contemporary shelving built into the recesses either side of a chimney breast. A good way to gain storage without losing floor space, and to hide pipework and wiring.

Alcoves

Media units

Living room storage built around the television, speakers and the boxes that come with them. Cable runs and ventilation are designed in rather than worked around afterwards.

Media units

Home offices

Anything from a fully fitted spare room to a desk and shelving under the stairs. Built to the dimensions of the room and the way you work.

Home offices

General carpentry

Second fix work, including hanging doors and under stairs storage.

General carpentry
How we work

Built in the workshop, not in your living room

Fitted furniture is often made on site, which means weeks of cutting, sanding and spraying in the room you are trying to live in. We do it the other way round. Your furniture is manufactured offsite at our workshop and brought to you finished, so the dust, the noise and the disruption are compressed into the installation instead of spread across the whole job.

From first call to finished

What happens when you get in touch

  1. You tell us what you are thinking about

    A phone call or an email with the room, roughly what you want and where you are.

  2. We come and measure

    We visit the house, look at the space, and talk through what will and will not work in it.

  3. We build it at the workshop

    Your furniture is made offsite, to the measurements we took.

  4. We fit it

    We install it ourselves, and we clear up after ourselves.
